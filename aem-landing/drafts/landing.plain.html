From Open to Work

What we believe

Work is changing, not ending.

Jobs change, some go and new ones come, as in past disruptions to work.

Human capability is becoming more important, not less.

As AI handles more routine tasks, judgment, creativity and connection carry more of the value.

Leaders have more agency than they think.

How work is redesigned is a choice, and that choice is being made right now.

The moment we're in

The conversation about AI and work is one of the most important of our time, and right now it is anchored around fear. Two beliefs drive that fear:

Belief

Fatalism

The sense that this moment is pre-determined, and that AI is in control, not us.

Belief

A fixed mindset about human capability

The idea that AI can keep getting better, but we can't.

Both are understandable. Neither is helpful, or true.

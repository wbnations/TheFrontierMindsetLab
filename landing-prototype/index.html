The Frontier Mindset Lab

Expanding human capability in the age of AI

Work is changing, not ending. The Frontier Mindset is how we learn, choose and create as AI keeps changing what work can be. The goal is simple: more capable people, not simply more capable AI.

Save your seat Explore the Frontier Mindset

The moment we're in

AI keeps getting better. The question is whether we will too. Two beliefs often get in the way:

Belief

Fatalism

the sense that this moment is pre-determined, and that AI is in control, not us.

Belief

A fixed mindset about human capability

the idea that AI can keep getting better, but we can't.

Both are understandable. Neither is helpful, or true.

People can keep getting better through practice. That's where the human side of AI transformation begins.

What is the Frontier Mindset?

Working definition

The Frontier Mindset is how we learn, choose and create as AI keeps changing what work can be.

It starts with using AI well: adapting as work changes and discerning where AI helps and where human judgment matters most. But it reaches beyond AI, to reimagining work and creating value that wasn't possible before. It isn't a technical skill or a destination. It's a way of working rooted in our human edge: the ability to keep getting better through practice. Anyone can build it, but no one builds it alone.

The mindsets in practice

Four working lenses for what the Frontier Mindset looks like day to day. They're still evolving, and we'll refine them as we learn.

Adapt

"I'm not fixed. I can learn and shape how I respond to change."

What it looks like: Tries a new approach on a bounded task, asks for feedback, adjusts.

Discern

"Fluency isn't truth. I question, weigh it and stay accountable."

What it looks like: Checks key claims, flags uncertainty, knows when not to use AI.

Create

"I can help shape what comes next."

What it looks like: Reframes a problem, prototypes a new workflow, tests and revises.

[Lead / Coach]

"My effectiveness includes what others become able to do."

What it looks like: Asks before prescribing, gives specific feedback, removes barriers.

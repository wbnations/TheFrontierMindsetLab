The moment we're in

Keep layoutChanged copy
The moment we're in

AI keeps getting better. The question is whether we will too. Many people feel three fears at once:

Fear
Using it too little
the worry of falling behind as the tools, and the work, move on.
Fear
Trusting it too much
the worry of handing over judgment that should stay human.
Fear
Being replaced by it
the longer-term worry that there won't be a place for people in the work.
Each fear is understandable. None of them has to decide what happens next.

People can keep getting better through practice. That's where the human side of AI transformation begins.

This isn't a promise that AI will work out on its own. Resetting around human potential and ambition is real work. Over the next decade, the systems that prepare people for work, employ them and help them build something new will need to be redesigned. The Lab is one place to start: practical, tested at Microsoft first, and honest about what we're still learning.

The Frontier Mindset: definition and spine

Changed copy
What is the Frontier Mindset? Working definition
The Frontier Mindset is how we learn, choose and create as AI keeps changing what work can be.

It isn't a technical skill or a destination, but a way of working rooted in our human edge: the ability to keep getting better through practice. Anyone can build it, but no one builds it alone.

The goal is simple: more capable people, not simply more capable AI.
01 · How to AI

Use AI well

Adapt as work changes, and use judgment about where and how AI contributes.

02 · How to human with AI

Reimagine what work can become

Look beyond adoption and efficiency toward different work, stronger human contribution and new value.

03 · Culture

Build capability through practice

People develop through doing and feedback. Leaders shape the culture, workflows and room to practice.
